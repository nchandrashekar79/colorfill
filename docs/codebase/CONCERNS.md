### 4) Performance and Scaling Concerns

| Concern | Evidence | Current symptom | Scaling risk | Suggested improvement |
|---------|----------|-----------------|--------------|-----------------------|

| Coverage marking is bounded but per-point | `game.js:1336-1388` (`markContact` iterates only the bounding rows/columns of the segment, clamped to the 32×32 grid) | None observed | Low — bounded by `COVERAGE_CELLS²` = 1024 cells per segment; `markContact` early-exits already-painted/mask-free cells | No action needed; the grid design deliberately avoids pixel readback (`README.md:106-108`) |
